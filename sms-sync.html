⚙️ How Phone SMS Sync Works
Method 1: Instant Mobile Web Companion (No APK)

Simply scan the QR code with your Android phone's camera. When you click "Send SMS" in the extension, the companion rings/vibrates on your phone and opens the pre-filled SMS in your default Messages app. Tap send on your Teletalk SIM!

Method 2: Android SMS Gateway APK (100% Silent Background)

Install the free, open-source Android SMS Gateway APK. It listens to the extension's REST webhook and automatically sends SMS from your Teletalk SIM and syncs replies completely in the background without needing to tap your screen!

Method 3: MacroDroid / Automate (Play Store)

Use MacroDroid to trigger on incoming SMS from 16222 and forward the payload to /api/sms/incoming via HTTP POST.
